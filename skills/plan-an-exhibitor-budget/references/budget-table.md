## Worked arithmetic (made-up inputs, to show the method)
Total 30,000; deal size 20,000; close rate from meeting to deal 0.1, so one meeting is worth 2,000; meetings needed
30,000 / 2,000 = 15. Three staff, 18 show hours, one qualified meeting per staff hour at best gives 54. The plan can
work if the floor brings the buyers; `vet-a-trade-show-before-exhibiting` checks that part.
