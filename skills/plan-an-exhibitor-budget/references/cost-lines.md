# Cost lines

| Line | What's in it | Where the number comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Space or package | booth space, or a sponsor package that includes space | `get_conference` packages; organizer's floor plan price list |
| Booth build or rental | structure, flooring, counters, storage, design | quotes from 2-3 builders or a rental kit |
| Graphics | printed panels, banners, screens | printer quote |
| Shipping and material handling | freight to and from the hall, handling by weight (often called drayage in the US) | exhibitor manual rates, freight quote |
| Show services | electrical, internet, furniture, cleaning, rigging, labor for install and dismantle | exhibitor manual order forms (early-order rates vs on-site) |
| Lead capture | badge scanner or app licenses | exhibitor manual |
| Promotion | pre-show email to your own list, giveaways, a sponsored session or meal | your own costs; package add-ons |
| Travel and hotel | flights, hotel nights, ground transport | travel quotes for the dates (prices rise near big shows) |
| Meals and hosting | team meals, a customer dinner | user's estimate |
| Staff days | days on site x people x loaded day rate | user's HR or finance number |
| Follow-up | sales time on post-show follow-up | user's estimate |
| Contingency | a share of the total the user sets | user's choice; say what it is |

Ask the user for their past show costs first; one real past invoice beats any benchmark.

Reference prices (ConferenceDB /sponsorship-pricing, read 2026-10-29, US-dollar packages): booth, table or exhibit
space median $3,500 (424 packages); Bronze $4,000 (532); Silver $6,000 (646); Gold $10,000 (794); Platinum $20,000
(1,021). They cover the package price only, none of the other lines.
