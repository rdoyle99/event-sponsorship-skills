# Prospectus structure (2-4 pages)

## Page 1: the room
- **Audience promise, one line.** "400 heads of payments and risk from US banks and credit unions, two days, one room."
- **Audience numbers.** Attendees, titles or seniority split, industries, countries, with the source in small type
  ("2026 badge scans", "ticket sales to 2026-09-30").
- **Last edition, if any.** Attendance, sessions, returning sponsors (from the organizer's records).
- **Category proof.** "Companies that sponsor events like this include ..." (8-12 names from `find_sponsors_for_event`,
  labeled as such).

## Page 2: the tiers
| Tier | Price | Available | Stage | Meetings | Space | Passes | Branding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then add-ons as a short priced list.

## Page 3: dates and how to buy
- Contract deadline, logo and copy deadline, booth build window, payment terms, cancellation terms.
- One contact role ("Sponsorship lead") with a role inbox or form. The organizer adds the person.

## Optional page 4
- Floor plan with booth numbers and which are sold.
- Session formats a sponsor can host.

## Formats
- PDF for email. Keep the file small enough to attach.
- A web page version helps sponsors forward it inside their company.
