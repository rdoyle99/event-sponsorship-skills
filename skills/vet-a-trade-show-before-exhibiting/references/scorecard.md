# Show scorecard

| Signal | How to count | Reads well | Reads badly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyers present | companies on the list the user sells to | several named target accounts | none, or only vendors |
| Competitors present | competitor domains on the list | 1-3 (the category shows up) | none (wrong show) or all of them in big booths (you'll be lost) |
| Category share | exhibitors in the user's category / exhibitors read | a clear section of the floor | 1 in 200 |
| Returning exhibitors | on both this and the previous edition's list / previous list | most come back | most don't |
| New exhibitors | `new_since_previous_edition` | some new, a growing show | the list is mostly new and the old one left |
| Editions on file | `editions_on_file` | 2+ to compare | 1: no return data, say so |
| Price published | `sponsorship_packages.published` | yes, with inclusions | no: use the benchmark and ask the organizer |

Thresholds depend on the category; there is no published standard. Show the raw counts and let the user weigh them.

## Verdicts
- **Go**: buyers present, category present, most exhibitors return, the cost fits the budget.
- **Go as a sponsor**: as above, and the floor is crowded with competitors, so a booth alone would vanish. Look for a
  session, a hosted meal or a meeting program.
- **Walk the floor**: buyers or partners present but the cost or crowd doesn't fit. Attend, book meetings, skip the booth.
- **Skip**: no buyers, or the list is mostly companies that didn't return.
