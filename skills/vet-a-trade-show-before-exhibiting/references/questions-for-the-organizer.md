# Questions to send the organizer before signing

Ask in writing and keep the answers with the decision.

1. Audited attendance for the last edition, and how it was counted (badge scans, unique registrants, paid).
2. The attendee job-title or industry split, as aggregates. Not names.
3. Which exhibitors renewed for this edition, as a count or share.
4. Booth location options and which are sold; a floor plan.
5. What lead capture is included (badge scanner, app), and its cost.
6. Exhibitor manual: build rules, shipping deadlines, required contractors, electrical and internet prices.
7. Cancellation and refund terms, and the payment schedule.
8. Speaking or session options and what they cost.

An organizer who won't answer 1 or 3 is telling you something; note it in the verdict.
